## Top 3 this period
1. **Medicare PFS CY2027 comment period closed Sept 14.** The proposed rule has no new dental proposals, so the current "inextricably linked" policy carries forward unless the final rule, usually out around Nov 1, changes it. [Federal Register](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/16/2026-14327/medicare-and-medicaid-programs-cy-2027-payment-policies-under-the-physician-fee-schedule-and-other)
2. **Two House hearings on Sept 14–15 touched dental insurance.** Energy & Commerce Health considered the DOC Access Act (H.R. 1521, non-covered services). House Judiciary looked at market concentration, network leasing, MA supplemental dental reporting and **AI/automated claims review transparency**, which is directly relevant to Overjet. *(Primary committee records not yet confirmed; see Watch list.)*
3. **CMS Medicaid Enterprise Systems IT Standards RFI (issued Aug 10):** ADA comments (reported Sept 22) push for dental data standards, FHIR readiness and EPSDT dental priority. This is relevant to interoperability and prior-auth strategy. *(RFI posted on sam.gov; see Watch list.)*
